# Drug-Related Crime Intelligence Dashboard – 2022

## Project Title
Drug-Related Crime Intelligence Dashboard – 2022

## Project Objectives
- To analyze drug-related crime incidents recorded in 2022.
- To uncover patterns in crime types, drug types, locations, age groups, and gender.
- To evaluate key performance indicators such as arrest rate, conviction rate, overdose rate, and hospital impact.
- To provide law enforcement and stakeholders with clear, data-driven insights for better resource allocation and intervention strategies.

## Dataset Used
Dataset: Drug-Related Crime Incidents 2022  
Contains incident-level data including crime type, drug type, location, age group, gender, arrest status, conviction status, overdose incidents, and hospital admissions.

## Key Questions Explored
1. What is the total number of drug-related crime incidents in 2022?
2. What are the most common crime types and drug types involved?
3. How do incidents vary across different locations (Urban, Downtown, Suburb, Rural)?
4. Which age groups are most affected?
5. What is the gender distribution across different drug types?
6. What are the arrest and conviction rates by drug type?
7. How significant is the overdose and hospital impact across drug types?
8. How did incident volume trend throughout the year?

## Process
1. **Data Cleaning and Preparation**
   - Standardized crime type, drug type, location, and age group categories.
   - Handled missing or inconsistent records.
   - Created calculated fields for rates (Arrest Rate, Conviction Rate, Overdose Rate, Hospital Rate).

2. **Data Analysis**
   - Aggregated total incidents and key rates.
   - Analyzed distribution by crime type, drug type, location, age, and gender.
   - Examined trends over time and comparative rates across drug categories.

## Dashboard Building
- Built an interactive intelligence dashboard using **Google Looker Studio**.
- Included key performance indicators (Total Incidents, Arrest Rate, Conviction Rate, Overdose Rate, Hospital Rate, Average Abuser Age).
- Created visualizations for Crime Type Distribution, Drug Type Distribution, Incidents Over Time, Location Analysis, Age Distribution, Gender vs Drug Type, Arrest & Conviction Rates, Overdose & Hospital Impact, and Police Activity.
- Added multiple filters (Crime Type, Drug Type, Location, Age Group, Treatment, Arrest Record, Overdose, Gender) for flexible exploration.

## Recommendations and Insights
1. **Crime Type Concentration**  
   Theft (3K) and Burglary (2.9K) are the most frequent crime types.  
   ➔ Prioritize preventive measures and patrol focus on theft and burglary-related drug crimes.

2. **Drug Type Distribution**  
   Marijuana, Cocaine, Methamphetamine, and Heroin are almost evenly distributed (≈25% each).  
   ➔ Maintain balanced enforcement and treatment programs across all major drug categories.

3. **Location Hotspots**  
   Urban and Downtown areas record the highest number of incidents.  
   ➔ Allocate more resources and community intervention programs to urban and downtown zones.

4. **Age Group Vulnerability**  
   The 26–35 age group accounts for the highest number of incidents (3.6K), followed by 36–45 (3.2K).  
   ➔ Design targeted awareness and rehabilitation programs for adults in their late 20s to mid-40s.

5. **Gender Pattern**  
   Males dominate across all drug types.  
   ➔ Develop gender-specific intervention and support programs, with stronger focus on male abusers.

6. **Arrest & Conviction Rates**  
   Arrest rates hover around 50% and conviction rates around 50–51% across drug types.  
   ➔ Review investigation and prosecution processes to improve conversion from arrest to conviction.

7. **Overdose & Hospital Impact**  
   Overdose rates are consistently high (≈50%) across all drug types.  
   ➔ Strengthen emergency response systems and expand access to overdose prevention resources (e.g., naloxone).

## Conclusion
This dashboard provides a comprehensive intelligence view of drug-related crimes in 2022. It enables law enforcement agencies and policymakers to quickly identify high-risk crime types, locations, age groups, and drug categories. The interactive design supports deeper exploration and helps turn complex crime data into actionable strategies for prevention, enforcement, and public health intervention.
